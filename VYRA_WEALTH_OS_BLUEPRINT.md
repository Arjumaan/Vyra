# VYRA WEALTH OS — ADVANCED WEALTH & FINANCIAL PLANNING ENGINE

## Product Enhancement & Implementation Specification

**Vision:** Evolving Vyra from an AI Expense Tracker into a comprehensive AI-powered Personal Wealth Operating System (Wealth OS).

> **Where they are today → where they are going → what they need to do → when they can achieve financial independence.**

---

## 1. FIRE & RETIREMENT ENGINE
Calculates "when can I become financially independent?" based on dynamic financial behavior.
- **Calculations:** Net Worth, Savings Rate, Retirement Expenses, FIRE Number, Safe Withdrawal Rate (configurable 3%-4.5%).
- **Scenarios:** Lean FIRE, Regular, Fat, Coast, Barista.
- **Simulation:** Real-time What-If simulator for income/expense/market changes.
- **AI Advisor:** Explainable insights on trajectory and required behavior modifications.

## 2. DEBT DESTRUCTION PLANNER
An active debt optimization engine.
- **Strategies:** Debt Snowball (lowest balance) and Debt Avalanche (highest interest), plus custom strategies.
- **Simulation:** Calculate interest saved and optimized debt-free dates based on extra monthly contributions.
- **AI Coach:** Recommendations distinguishing mathematical projections from actuals.

## 3. REAL ESTATE PORTFOLIO & YIELD MANAGER
Professional real-estate wealth management.
- **Profiles:** Residential, Commercial, Land, Vacation, etc.
- **Metrics:** Gross Yield, Net Yield, Cap Rate, Cash-on-Cash Return.
- **Management:** Mortgage Amortization schedules, Tenant/Lease tracking, Alerts.
- **AI Insights:** Underperforming assets, refinancing scenarios, cash-flow trends.

## 4. SIDE-HUSTLE & MICRO-BUSINESS LEDGER
Separates personal and business finances while maintaining a global Net Worth view.
- **Ledger:** Independent income/expense tracking for freelancers, LLCs, content creators.
- **Dashboard:** P&L, Profit Margin, Accounts Receivable/Payable.
- **Tax Prep:** Mark transactions for deductibility and tax categorization.
- **AI Insights:** Unprofitable clients, revenue trends, expense spikes.

## 5. LEGACY & ESTATE PLANNING
A secure module for organizing digital legacy and instructions.
- **Vault:** Wills, trusts, policies, encryption at rest/transit.
- **Beneficiaries:** Documentation (non-legally binding) of asset distribution.
- **Trusted Contacts:** Role-based access (View Only, Emergency, Legacy).
- **Dead Man's Switch:** Time-locked release workflow with multi-verification grace periods. Cryptographic key splitting (zero-trust).

## 6. UNIFIED VYRA WEALTH OS
Centralized Wealth Intelligence Layer connecting Cashflow → Debt → Assets → FIRE → Legacy.

## 7. AI WEALTH INTELLIGENCE LAYER
Top-level AI copilot understanding the full financial context.
- **Explainability:** Insight → Why → Data Used → Assumptions → Impact → Action.

## 8. FINANCIAL SIMULATION ENGINE
Reusable layer for Monte Carlo and deterministic forecasting across all modules.

## 9. DATA ARCHITECTURE
Normalized architecture with performant read models for entities (Transaction, Asset, Liability, Tenant, LegacyDocument, etc.).

## 10. FINANCIAL DATA INTEGRITY
- High-precision decimal arithmetic (no floating-point money).
- Atomic, auditable, timestamped, idempotent transactions.

## 11. SMART ALERT SYSTEM
Intelligent notifications across FIRE, Debt, Real Estate, Business, and Legacy modules.

## 12. PRIVACY & SECURITY
- Encryption at rest and in transit.
- Zero-trust architecture, MFA, least-privilege authorization.
- Cryptographic inheritance mechanisms for the Legacy Vault.

## 13. RESPONSIBLE FINANCIAL INTELLIGENCE
Clear communication that Vyra is a planning tool, not a licensed fiduciary.

---
**Core Philosophy:** Track → Understand → Optimize → Plan → Predict → Grow → Protect → Transfer.
